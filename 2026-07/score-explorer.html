Score explorer

What this does

Pure score arithmetic — it ignores the board. On move N the score either +N (level move), ×N (up) or ÷N (down, only when it divides evenly). Altitude is 0 or 1, so:

standing onallowedresult
ground +N or ×N stays ground / rises to tower
tower +N or ÷N stays tower / drops to ground

So × and ÷ must alternate, with any run of + between them — you can never multiply twice or divide twice without the other in between. Anything this finds still has to be realisable on the board; check it in the sandbox.

Set the inputs and press Search.
