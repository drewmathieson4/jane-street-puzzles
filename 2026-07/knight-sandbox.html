Rules reference

Each of the 13 regions gets exactly one tower (a second cube on one of its squares). A square's altitude is 0 (ground) or 1 (tower). The knight starts on a1 with score 0 and never revisits a square.

A move travels 0 units in one dimension, 1 in another and 2 in the third. Altitude only spans 0–1, so a ±2 altitude change is impossible and exactly two move families remain:

typeshapealtitudescore on move N
level ordinary knight moveboth ends the samescore += N
up straight 2 orthogonallyground → towerscore ×= N
down straight 2 orthogonallytower → ground score ÷= N, only if even

So a knight on a tower can only make an ordinary knight move to another tower, and up/down moves are rook-like two-steppers. Score starts at 0, and 0 divides by anything.

Scores were recorded at moves 0, 3, 6, …, 18, then every K moves for some K > 3. A square shows a number iff the knight was on it at a recording moment. Answer = for every unvisited square, sum the scores of its orthogonally adjacent on-path squares; add those sums up.
