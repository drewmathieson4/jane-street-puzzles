Rules baked in

Move N from a square at altitude a:
• same altitude → score + N
• up (ground→tower) → score × N
• down (tower→ground) → score ÷ N, only if N divides the score

Altitude is 0 or 1, so ups and downs must alternate: never two × in a row without a ÷ between them, and never two ÷ in a row. Level moves happen at either altitude and don't change it. Max move number 63 (64 squares). At most 13 tower squares exist, one per region.
